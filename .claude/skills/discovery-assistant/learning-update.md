# Communicate

Draft a short learning update for stakeholders who do not follow the board. It builds only on the cards, links to each one, and is never sent by you.

## Checklist

```
- [ ] 1. Settle the period and the audience
- [ ] 2. Read the board
- [ ] 3. Draft the update
- [ ] 4. Check every line against its card
- [ ] 5. Show the draft and what was left out
```

**1. Period and audience.** The default is the last 7 days, for stakeholders outside the team. Ask if the person wants another period or one milestone.

**2. Read the board** as in `SKILL.md`. Use cards closed as completed in the period, cards whose Status or Outcome changed in the period (from `updatedAt`, an estimate), and the cards in *Investigating* and *Informed bet* now.

Leave out a card in *What we currently know* whose *Current understanding* section is empty, and list it separately in step 5.

**3. Draft the update:**

```markdown
## Learning update, <period>

**What we learned**
- <the understanding, as the card's title states it>. <one line on what it means>. ([#12](<url>))

**What changed**
- We believed <old belief>. It did not hold: <what we understand now>. ([#15](<url>))

**What remains uncertain**
- <important open questions or beliefs>, and the informed bets we proceed on: <bet>, revisited <date>. ([#9](<url>))

**What we are learning now**
- <investigating cards>. ([#18](<url>))

**What this affects**
- <decisions linked from the cards>
```

Skip empty sections. Keep each line to what the card says, in the team's words. Present *Did not hold* as a good result: the team avoided building on something that was not true.

For one milestone instead of a period, use the milestone's three parts: what the desired learning was, what is now understood, and whether «reached when» is met, according to the cards.

**4. Check every line against its card.** The understanding, numbers and scope must appear on the card. Remove anything that does not.

**5. Show the draft**, and list separately any cards left out for a missing conclusion.

## Never

- Never send, post or publish the update. A person reviews and sends it.
- Never present an informed bet as knowledge, or something still being investigated as settled.
- Never make an understanding broader than its card, round numbers, or soften a *Did not hold*.
- Never include names, identifying quotes or details from raw sources.
- Never use sales language.
